How it works

  1. Gemini reads the handwriting

    Your photo goes to Google's Gemini model, which transcribes it line by line and marks anything unclear instead of guessing.

  2. Rules read Indian and US notation

    Pattern rules find strength, how often, timing and duration, including 1-0-1, BD, TDS, SOS, x 5/7, Sig lines, #30 and refills.

  3. Brands are linked to generics

    Names are checked against a list of common Indian brands, then the US RxNorm database. Generics show in both forms, like paracetamol and acetaminophen.

Check every line against the original

Handwriting recognition can misread a drug name or a number, and a wrong dose could be harmful if followed. Confirm with the prescriber or a pharmacist before acting on anything here. This site is not medical advice.

The Indian brand list is a starter list of common brands, not every product, and brand variants can differ in strength or ingredients. Your photo is sent through this site's server to Google's Gemini API, and this site does not store it. Google may use content sent on its free tier to improve its products, so don't upload prescriptions you want kept private.
